## Ordered GDR work

1. **Qualified point-only refits with numeric result contracts.** Restrict the
   first route to the declared single-primary Poisson/half-normal domain with
   the established removal law and normalized Gaussian observer/abundance
   effects. Reuse the private exact-thinning and observation-preparation work.
   Qualify the finite-Poisson-CDF correction at actual designs and tail-sensitive
   candidates so the native finite-K target is retained. Agreement of an
   untruncated approximation at a few admitted points does not qualify all
   exploratory candidates. Keep engine/target changes explicit.

   Numeric packets need fixed/log-SD coordinates, objective/marginal score,
   current latent modes, scaled stationarity/curvature diagnostics, provider/
   numeric design/response identities and compact original/cold/final attempts.
   State whether covariance is computed, deferred or unavailable. Numeric points
   must not masquerade as native S4 fits. Reconstruct executable objectives from
   numeric states in each process; do not serialize tapes. Preserve starts,
   highest-likelihood eligibility and every failed/lower/unknown attempt.

   Restore fn/gr at the exact admitted optimizer point before final reports,
   modes and curvature. Require full final fixed covariance, positive current-
   mode latent precision and joint fixed/latent density uncertainty. Deferring
   covariance cannot turn a failed fit into an eligible result. Benchmark all
   equivalent preparation, fitting, uncertainty and qualification work, keeping
   warm-cache, cold-cache and restart costs separate.

2. **Reusable paired GDR prediction and spatial-fold jobs.** Freeze training-
   only standardization, actual native knots/boundaries/basis columns and starts,
   including unstandardized optimized covariates and native knot-adjustment
   warnings. Integrate Gaussian states under an explicit approximation. Map
   known groups by verified labels and integrate unknown observer/abundance
   states. Unused normalized priors must integrate to one. Setting unknown
   random effects to zero is a different estimand.

   A qualified implementation may use joint-minus-training marginal log
   likelihood with fixed training coefficients. Held-out responses define the
   probability being scored; they do not choose transformations or starts.
   Label plug-in versus parameter-integrated scores, preserve failures, fold
   membership, support/extrapolation and numeric restart identities. Existing
   three unbuffered folds stay regression/exploratory evidence; future buffered
   validation needs a separate declared scientific design.

3. **Scaled diagnostics, target metadata and supported initial fitting.** Add
   scaled scores or a Newton decrement with thresholds and coordinate conventions
   in the audit, preserving current-mode Hessian checks. Label native-margin and
   paired first-encounter response encodings. Expose their data-only factorial
   constant and survey IDs consistently when comparing representations. The
   reported omitted constant was an analysis-driver bookkeeping error, not a
   refitter defect; do not selectively shift likelihoods to improve rankings.

   Qualify numeric-only preparation/snapshot reconstruction and active constructor
   layouts without retaining an inactive beta_phi coordinate. Keep
   gdr_start_values() as the normal route from a live fit. A public controlled
   initial-fit route should check providers before expensive work and forward
   evaluated controls, allowing users to avoid private constructor access.

4. **Evidence-led flexibility after the first three items.** Unpenalized
   low-dimensional natural splines already work. Penalized GDR smooths require
   estimating penalty strength inside the same likelihood and propagating it
   through selection, prediction, validation and uncertainty. Independent 50-km
   Gaussian abundance blocks are not a continuous correlated spatial field.
   A future field must retain the GDR observation process and pass residual,
   held-out, identifiability and computational checks. Neither extension resolves
   the reported robin/vireo removal-time mismatch or identifies absolute all-sex
   density. Keep current qualified response shapes as the benchmark.
