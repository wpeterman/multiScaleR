# Working-file limits for CV validation

The November 3 resume requires active control of temporary-file growth.
Keep the existing numerical, process and disk reserves. This policy does not
authorize deleting unique inputs/results or editing frozen historical producers.

- Create source candidates from the curated source manifest. Do not copy the
  entire checkout, .git, old installed libraries, build caches or the fourteen
  build-ignored legacy example fits into a new candidate.
- Reuse each exact archive and its installed library for that batch's full and
  focused installed checks. Retain one durable accepted archive rather than
  making copies for each test context. Preserve failed evidence and identities.
- Keep fold fits and numerical intermediates in memory. Checkpoints contain
  compact scores, diagnostics, identities and test results, not model chains,
  AD tapes or arrays of candidate fits. Full CV fits remain opt-in for users.
- Record sizes of the explicitly owned candidate, validation workspace and
  output folder at batch boundaries. Distinguish logical size, file allocation
  and volume free space. A logical duplicate total does not prove net physical
  recovery during concurrent writes or delayed deallocation.
- Investigate unexpected growth before another producer starts. Existing
  admission controls still require20GiB free for launch, stable30GiB before
  build and5GiB at runtime. Known foreign jobs remain untouched.
- After owned processes close and acceptance is recorded, compiled source-tree
  objects may be removed only from an exact allowlist of newly owned paths,
  when they are excluded from every frozen source/test/installed identity map.
  Retain installed DLLs, source, archive, logs and all failed numerical results.
- Other cleanup requires an exact path list, retained canonical originals,
  matching hashes and a restoration record. Never broadly clear Temp or Rtmp,
  another agent's workspace, scientific archives or protected system files.

The earlier duplicate cleanup is recorded in disk_audit24. Its canonical
originals remain. Old temporary-tree manifests that included removed duplicate
inputs need restoration before reuse; closed producers must not be replayed.
